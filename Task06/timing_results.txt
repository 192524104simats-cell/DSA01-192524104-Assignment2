Q6 – Inline and Non-Inline Timing Results

Number of function calls: 10,000,000

Without optimization (-O0):
Inline time: 73969 microseconds
Non-inline time: 58534 microseconds

With optimization (-O2):
Inline time: 32841 microseconds
Non-inline time: 25789 microseconds

Both versions produced the same result: 2400000400.

Observation:
Optimization reduced the execution time of both versions.
The non-inline version was faster in this experiment.
Inline functions do not always guarantee better performance.
The actual result depends on compiler optimization and function-call overhead.
